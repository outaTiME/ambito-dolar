# Device frames

The bezels `compose.ts`, `feature-graphic.ts` and `website-phone.ts` draw, with their geometry in
`config.ts`, measured with `node measure-frame.ts <png>`.

- `18-pro-max-black.png` (iPhone 18 Pro Max, Black), `ipad-pro-m5-11-space-black-landscape.png`
  (iPad Pro M5 11", Space Black, landscape) and `duo-outer-night-sky.png` (iPhone Duo, Night Sky,
  outer display) are Apple's Product Bezels from
  [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/), Copyright Apple Inc.
  Apple licenses them for mock-ups of apps on Apple platforms only, so they frame the iOS devices
  and never an android one.
- `android-compact-black.png` and `android-expanded-black.png` are the black Compact and Expanded
  frames of the Figma community file
  [Android Compact, Medium, and Expanded Device Frames](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1425969168466168269/android-compact-medium-and-expanded-device-frames),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), exported with the screen left
  transparent.

None of them falls under the repo license, each keeps the one above.
